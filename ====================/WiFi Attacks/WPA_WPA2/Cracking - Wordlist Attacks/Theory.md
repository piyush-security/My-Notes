## Theory 📜📰 📜
- As said before, **WPA**'s were not useful as they do not contain any info that can be used to crack.
- The only packets which can be used in cracking is **handshake packets**. 🤝

- Everytime a client connects to the AP, A **FOUR Way Handsheke** occurs. 🤝
- By capturing the handshake, we can use **aircrack-ng**'s wordlist attack to get the key.🔑

## Conclusion 🏁🧰

To crack a **WPA**/**WPA2** AP with **WPS Disabled** we need two things : 
- Capture the Handshake. 🤝
- A Wordlist. ⚔️
