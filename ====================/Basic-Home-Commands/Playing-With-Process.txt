# Killing Process. 

### Find Process : 

```sh
ps aux | grep "process_name" | grep -v grep

```

### Killing : 

```sh 
#Kill all processes by name
pkill -f process_name

#Kill multiple matching processes at once:
ps -ef | grep "keyword" | awk '{print $2}' | xargs kill -9


#Interactive kill (choose from menu):
ps -eo pid,cmd --sort=-%cpu | head -20 | fzf | awk '{print $1}' | xargs kill -9

```
